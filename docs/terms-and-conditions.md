## 7. Predictions and Scoring

### How Scoring Works
- Before each match, users submit predictions on various scenarios (e.g., match winner, top scorer, total sixes)
- Predictions must be submitted before the deadline (default: 45 minutes before match start)
- After the deadline, predictions are locked and visible to all squad members
- Outcomes are resolved based on match data from third-party cricket APIs, or manually by squad administrators
- Points are awarded for correct predictions

### You Acknowledge That:
- Match data may be delayed by several minutes compared to live television broadcasts
- Data may occasionally be incomplete, inaccurate, or unavailable due to third-party API limitations
- Automated scoring is based on third-party data and may contain errors
- Manual scoring decisions by squad administrators are final within their squad
- We do not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or timeliness of any match data
- **No prediction outcome, leaderboard position, or award entitles you to any compensation, reward, prize, or monetary consideration**
- Points, ranks, and leaderboard positions exist solely for entertainment and bragging rights

### Tiebreaking
When two users have the same score for a match, the user who submitted their predictions earlier is ranked higher.
