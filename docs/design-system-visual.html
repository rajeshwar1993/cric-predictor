Copy & Voice

Direct, confident, competitive, casual. Short sentences. No hedging.

Do "You're in!"
Don't "You have successfully joined."
Do "Predictions locked"
Don't "The prediction window has closed."
Do "3 of 19 picked"
Don't "You have completed 3 out of 19."
Do "Wrong call."
Don't "Unfortunately, your prediction..."
Do "Nailed it."
Don't "Great job! Your prediction was..."
Do "Dead last."
Don't "You are currently in the last..."

Accessibility

WCAG AA minimum. Touch targets, focus states, color independence.

48px
Touch Targets
Minimum 48x48px for all interactive elements. 8px gap minimum between targets.

2px solid brand color, 2px offset. Never hidden, always styled.

Color + Icon

Contrast Ratios
